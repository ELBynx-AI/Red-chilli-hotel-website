THE HOUSE SPECIALS

Three ways to treat a porotta.

Most places make one. We make all three, and they are the reason people drive out to Munchirai.

01

Kizhi Porotta

Torn by hand, packed with masala meat, tied into a parcel and put back on the heat. You open it at the table and the steam comes out first.

02

Nool Porotta

The same dough pulled into fine threads, then pressed back together. Soft, light, and it soaks up curry like nothing else.

03

Kothu Porotta

Chopped on the hot iron with egg, onion and masala. You hear it before you see it.

TRY IT

Press and hold to tear it open.

Hold the parcel. Let go and it closes again.

  1. Layers first.
  2. Then the masala.
  3. Then the steam.

That is a kizhi porotta.

STRAIGHT ANSWERS

The questions people actually ask.

Is it very oily?

Porotta needs oil to layer, there is no way around that. What we control is that it comes off the iron and goes straight to your table, never into a warming tray.

How spicy is it?

Kerala spicy. Say the word when you order and the kitchen will hold back the chilli.

Is there anything without meat?

Plenty. Puttu, idli, idiyappam, appam, dosa, chappathi and the vegetable meals are all made without it.

Do you do parcel?

Yes. Say so at the counter and we will pack it hot for you to carry home.

Can I get food for a function?

Yes. Weddings, church functions, school programmes and house events. Porotta and biryani in quantity. Come and see us with the numbers and the date and we will sort it out.

When are you open?

Every day, six in the morning until half past ten at night.
